Privacy Policy

Effective 25 October 2026

Nguyen Thanh provides Pixel March: Ant Puzzle. Contact: globalaccess.vn@gmail.com.

Game data on your device

The game stores progress, settings, coins, inventory, purchase entitlements and transaction identifiers locally to operate the game and prevent duplicate rewards. It does not require an account or provide its own cloud sync. Device backups managed by Apple may include app data according to your device settings.

Advertising and service providers

The game uses Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation and Google AdMob for rewarded and interstitial advertising. These providers may collect IP address and approximate location, device and advertising identifiers, app and advertising interactions, and diagnostic or performance information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and operate their services. Data use depends on your device settings, permissions, region and privacy choices. We do not request precise location, contacts, camera or microphone access.

Where Apple’s tracking permission applies, the game asks before requesting access to the advertising identifier for tracking. You can decline and change the choice in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Where required, a privacy message lets you make advertising choices; a Privacy Choices option is available in the game’s Settings when required by the consent provider.

Provider details: Unity/ironSource privacy policy, Unity game player privacy policy, and Google Privacy Policy. Providers may process data in other countries and retain data under their policies.

In-app purchases

Apple processes payments. The game receives product information, transaction identifiers and purchase entitlements to deliver items, restore eligible purchases and prevent duplicate delivery. To verify and deliver purchases, the game sends purchase transaction information and an installation identifier to our purchase verification service hosted by Cloudflare. We do not receive your card number or payment credentials. See Apple’s privacy information.

Support communications

If you email support, we receive the email address and information you choose to send. We use it to respond and resolve the request, retain it as needed for support and applicable obligations, and do not use it for advertising.

Your choices and requests

You can change tracking permissions in iOS Settings and advertising privacy choices within the game when available. Removing the app removes its local game data from the device, subject to Apple-managed backups. For requests about support emails or privacy, contact us. For data held independently by advertising providers or Apple, use their privacy request channels. Applicable rights may include access, correction or deletion depending on your location.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through support, contact us so we can address it.

Policy changes

We will update this page and its effective date when our practices change.
