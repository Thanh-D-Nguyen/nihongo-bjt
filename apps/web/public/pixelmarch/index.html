Pixel March: Ant Puzzle

Support for the ant sorting puzzle game by Nguyen Thanh.

Contact support

Email globalaccess.vn@gmail.com. Include your game version, iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and a description of the problem. A screenshot can help. Do not send passwords, payment card details, or authentication codes.

Purchases and restoration

Purchases are processed by Apple. For missing non-consumable purchases, open the game’s Settings and choose Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase. Consumable coin packs and spent items are not restored by that button. Contact support if a completed purchase was not delivered.

To request a refund, use Apple’s Report a Problem. Apple handles payment and refund requests.

Ads

Rewarded ads are optional and require an internet connection and available advertising inventory. Rewards are delivered after the ad provider confirms completion. Remove Ads purchases remove automatic interstitial ads; optional rewarded ads remain available. If an ad does not load, try again later.

Progress

Game progress is stored on your device. The game does not require an account and does not provide its own cloud sync. Deleting the app may remove progress.
